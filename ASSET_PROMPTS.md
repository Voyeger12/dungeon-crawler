# Asset prompts

Die folgenden Produktionsassets wurden mit OpenAIs eingebauter Bildgenerierung im eingebauten Toolmodus erstellt. Chroma-Key-Atlanten wurden anschließend lokal freigestellt, zugeschnitten und als WebP optimiert.

## Versiegeltes Menütor

```text
Use case: production asset
Asset type: wide 16:9 main-menu background for a dark medieval browser dungeon brawler
Primary request: Create a monumental sealed dungeon gate inside an ancient underground stone entrance, designed as the backdrop for the Rune Deep main menu.
Scene/backdrop: a massive worm-eaten double oak gate reinforced with blackened hammered iron, surrounded by rough soot-stained stone blocks, broken chains, old brass rivets, shallow carved runes, and two very dim wall braziers.
Style/medium: highly polished hand-painted dark-fantasy game background, realistic materials with restrained stylization matching a gritty medieval reliquary interface.
Composition/framing: wide cinematic 16:9 composition, straight-on symmetrical view, gate centered, strong readable silhouette. Keep the central and lower-middle areas visually calm enough for separate DOM title and buttons; decorative detail concentrated toward edges and gate frame.
Lighting/mood: near-dark underground atmosphere, faint warm ember light from the sides, cold darkness in the doorway seams, dusty air, oppressive and ancient, not colorful.
Color palette: soot black, charcoal stone, dark walnut, oxblood-brown, old dull brass, restrained ember orange.
Materials/textures: cracked oak, chipped wrought iron, grime, ash, damp stone, worn leather straps, no polished surfaces.
Constraints: no characters, no creatures, no weapons, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no watermark, no UI boxes, no bright central light. Must remain readable behind high-contrast separate interface elements.
Avoid: clean fantasy castle, blue magic glow, neon, glossy gold, modern cinematic lens flare, overly busy center, photoreal photograph, cartoon style.
```

## Drei Runensegen-Tafeln

```text
Use case: production asset
Asset type: transparent-ready sprite atlas for three level-up choice cards in a dark medieval dungeon brawler
Primary request: On one perfectly flat pure chroma-green (#00FF00) background, create three separate tall runestone tablet frames for selectable blessings.
Subject: three distinct but related empty vertical tablets: one cracked dark stone with blackened iron corners, one worm-eaten dark oak tablet with old brass rivets, and one oxblood leather-covered reliquary tablet with bone and wrought-iron edging. Each must have a large calm empty central inset for separate DOM icon, name and description.
Style/medium: highly detailed hand-painted dark-fantasy game UI sprites, realistic battered medieval materials, matching a soot-dark reliquary interface.
Composition/framing: three complete front-facing orthographic tablets arranged horizontally, identical outer dimensions and silhouette family, generously separated by green gaps, no overlap, no perspective, no cast shadows outside silhouettes. Keep at least 8% green margin around every tablet.
Lighting/mood: dim diffuse neutral light, matte surfaces, grimy and ancient.
Color palette: charcoal, dark walnut, dried oxblood, dirty bone, dull brass, rust-brown; restrained contrast.
Constraints: background exactly uniform #00FF00 with no gradient, texture, shadow or spill. No text, letters, numbers, runes, icons, logos, watermark, gems, neon, magical glow. Subjects fully opaque with crisp edges for chroma-key removal.
Avoid: playing-card proportions, clean modern frames, glossy gold, ornate bright fantasy, skull centerpiece, weapons, cropped objects.
```

## Dungeonboden

```text
Use case: production asset
Asset type: seamless square top-down dungeon floor texture for a dark medieval browser dungeon brawler
Primary request: Create one seamless orthographic texture of ancient dungeon flagstones viewed perfectly from above.
Surface: irregular large blue-gray and charcoal stone slabs, cracked mortar, ground-in soot, damp stains, sparse grit, subtle worn traffic marks, and occasional tiny dried oxblood discoloration.
Style/medium: highly polished hand-painted dark-fantasy game texture with realistic battered medieval materials and restrained stylization.
Composition/framing: square texture, edge-to-edge surface only, perfectly tileable on all four edges, uniform scale suitable for repeating across large dungeon rooms. No perspective and no focal object.
Lighting/mood: very dim diffuse neutral ambient light baked evenly across the tile; avoid directional shadows so repetition remains unobtrusive.
Color palette: charcoal, cold blue-gray, soot black, desaturated brown, extremely restrained dried oxblood.
Constraints: no walls, no doors, no furniture, no props, no characters, no creatures, no weapons, no text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no UI, no border, no vignette, no watermark. Must tile seamlessly left-to-right and top-to-bottom.
Avoid: checkerboard regularity, bright highlights, colorful moss, glowing cracks, lava, magic runes, clean modern paving, photographic scan.
```

```text
Color palette: almost-black charcoal, cold gray, soot black, desaturated rust-brown.

Avoid: brick house wall, bright moss, glowing cracks, clean castle masonry, modern concrete, high saturation, photographic scan.
```

## Dungeon-Requisitenatlas

```text
Use case: production asset
Asset type: transparent-ready top-down environment prop sprite atlas for a dark medieval browser dungeon brawler
Primary request: On one perfectly flat pure chroma-green (#00FF00) background, create eight separate dungeon prop sprites, all seen from the same high three-quarter top-down game camera.
Subjects, arranged as a clean 4 by 2 grid in this exact order:
top row: a cracked squat basalt pillar; a reinforced worm-eaten oak crate; a bound dark oak barrel; a low pile of broken stone rubble.
bottom row: a square rusted iron spike trap; a closed battered treasure chest with dull brass bands; the same style treasure chest open with dark interior and restrained gold glint; a knee-high ancient rune shrine made of cracked stone, bone tokens and one very faint ember-red rune.
Style/medium: polished hand-painted dark-fantasy 2D game sprites, realistic battered medieval materials with restrained stylization, consistent with a gritty soot-dark dungeon.
Composition/framing: eight complete isolated objects, consistent camera angle and lighting, one object centered in each equal cell, generous green gaps, no overlap, no crop, no ground plane. Keep at least 5% green clearance around each sprite.
Lighting/mood: dim warm-neutral light from upper left, modest contact shading contained tightly under each object, matte dirty surfaces.
Color palette: charcoal, dark walnut, dirty bone, rust-brown, dull brass, tiny restrained oxblood/ember accent only on shrine.
Constraints: background exactly uniform #00FF00 with no gradient, texture, vignette or green shadows. No characters, creatures, weapons, text, letters, numbers, UI, logos, watermark, blue magic, neon. Subjects fully opaque except natural holes and with crisp silhouettes for chroma-key removal.
Avoid: inventory icons, individual framed boxes, glossy treasure, bright gold coins, cute cartoon proportions, inconsistent perspective, photoreal photograph.
```
